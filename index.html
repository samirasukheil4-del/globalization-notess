📘 Globalization, SAPs & Liberalization | Form Four NECTA Focus

🌍 Definition

Globalization is the process of increasing interconnection between countries through trade, investment, technology, and cultural exchange. It transforms the world into a global village.

⚙️ Driving Forces

  • Technological advances
  • Trade liberalization
  • International investments
  • Transport revolution
  • Global media & culture

🔍 Aspects

  • Economic
  • Political
  • Socio-cultural
  • Technological
  • Environmental

✅ Positive Effects on Tanzania

  • Increased foreign investments
  • Access to global markets (coffee, cashew nuts)
  • Growth of mobile money (M-Pesa)
  • Job creation by MNCs
  • International scholarships
  • Better healthcare access

⚠️ Negative Effects on Tanzania

  • Deindustrialization
  • Cultural erosion
  • Wealth inequality
  • Environmental degradation
  • Brain drain
  • Dependence on foreign aid

🏛️ Structural Adjustment Programs (SAPs)

Definition: SAPs were policies by IMF/World Bank (1980s-90s) requiring reduced government role.

Criticism

  • Increased poverty
  • Reduced social spending
  • Job losses
  • Loss of sovereignty

Achievements

  • Reduced inflation
  • Attracted foreign investment
  • Export diversification
  • Stabilized economy

📈 Economic Liberalization

Definition: Reducing government regulations to allow free market forces. In Tanzania: removal of price controls, privatization, financial reforms.
